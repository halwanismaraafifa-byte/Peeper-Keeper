About the PeeperKeeper Clip

A wearable clip that helps drivers stay awake. Its AI runs on the device and analyzes in real time, so the clip sounds an alarm at the first sign of microsleep, without any face images being stored.

Audible alarmSounds at the first sign
AI on the deviceAnalysis stays local
No faces storedPrivacy by design

How it works

  1. 1

    Clip it on. Attach the clip to the driver’s collar. It watches for early signs of microsleep.

  2. 2

    Camera double-checks. When the clip senses a possible microsleep, the camera wakes up so the AI can confirm whether it’s real.

  3. 3

    Alarm, then forget. The clip sounds an alarm right away. Analysis happens live on the device and no face images are saved.
